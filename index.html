Jeep Colors 1972-1986

This started as a way to pick a color for my rusty CJ-7. The published lists disagree, and there are few authoritative sources. Factory service manuals and dealer brochures are the strongest paper evidence; firewall tags would be better, but nobody can collect them all. Surviving AMC paint brochures do not separate AMC colors from Jeep colors, and supplier chips are not authoritative. Olenik and PaintRef.com are useful but conflict with each other—and PaintRef sometimes conflicts with itself by year versus by color. So this is one list with a confidence grade per year, based on the quality of the sources behind it. Sources: www.paintref.com, Olenik, and available PDF FSMs and brochures.

How to Use

  • Click an image to search photos of that color
  • Click the PPG link for paint-code details
  • Use the year menu to show only that model year
  • Use − / + to collapse or expand a row
